---
title: The case for small projects
description: Finishing something small beats planning something big.
date: 2026-11-14
---

Big projects stall because they ask you to be certain before you start. Small projects ask only that you begin.

## Why small works

- You finish, and finishing is a skill that compounds.
- Feedback arrives sooner, so the next attempt is better.
- The cost of being wrong is low.

```js
const ship = (idea) => idea.scope('small').then(publish);
```

Pick one idea this week and make the smallest version of it.
